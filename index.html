01

The assignment

任務

Meet Rin Sakuraba. Nineteen. First week at the Hoshimachi Herald.

Her editor gave her one notebook and one rule.

"Ask real people. Write down what they really think."

No experts. No press releases. Just a lifeguard, a fisherman, a grandmother, a kid on a bench. People who never get asked.

That is the whole game. You walk. You ask. You listen. Then you print it.
